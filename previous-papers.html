How to add a paper

  1. Open the official PaperHub link.
  2. Choose regulation, branch, semester and subject.
  3. Download the paper from the official source.
  4. Before uploading a paper into this app, confirm its regulation, exam session and subject code.

Future app updates can add verified PDF records to a paper catalogue without changing the calculator.
