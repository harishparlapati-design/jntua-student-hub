Privacy Policy

JNTUA Student Hub
Last updated: 28 October 2026

JNTUA Student Hub is an independent student utility intended to help students calculate and review semester SGPA and overall CGPA. It is not an official website of Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Anantapur (JNTUA).

Information the website may use

  • Google account information: If you choose Google sign-in, the app may receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture to identify the signed-in account in the interface.
  • Academic information: Information you enter, such as subjects, credits, grades, semester results, SGPA, and CGPA.
  • Backup information: If you choose Google Drive backup or restore, the app uses the Google Drive permissions shown on Google's consent screen to create or access its backup file.

Where information is stored

Academic information may be saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. If you use Google Drive backup, a backup may also be saved in your own Google Drive. Browser data does not automatically follow you to another device. Use backup/restore if available and check that it completed successfully.

Do not use this website to store passwords, government identification numbers, payment information, or other highly sensitive information.

How information is used

Information is used to calculate SGPA/CGPA, display your saved academic records and signed-in profile, and perform backup or restore actions you request.

Google services

If you use Google sign-in or Drive features, Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can review and revoke this app's access in your Google Account security settings. Review the permissions shown by Google before approving them.

Sharing and selling

JNTUA Student Hub is not intended to sell personal information or use academic records for advertising. Information stored in your browser or Drive is subject to your device and Google Account settings. GitHub Pages may process ordinary technical request data under GitHub's policies.

Retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored website data through your browser settings and delete backups from Google Drive. Signing out does not necessarily delete local data or Drive backups. Save a copy of anything you want to keep before deleting data.

Security and limitations

No website or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your Google Account secure, sign out on shared devices, and avoid using shared/public devices for private academic records. Check that backups and restores work as expected.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features change. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the website maintainer through the contact method published on the JNTUA Student Hub website or its associated GitHub repository.

JNTUA Student Hub is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by JNTUA unless expressly stated.
